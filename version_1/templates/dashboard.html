📘 Glossary
Zombie Waste
Spend on resources that behave like anomalies in cost versus usage, likely idle or over-provisioned.
EffectiveCost
The standardized spend metric from your billing data that this app uses for detection and forecasting.
BAU Forecast
Business-as-usual projection of the next 7 days of EffectiveCost if you make no changes.
Optimized Forecast
Projected EffectiveCost for the next 7 days assuming you remove the identified zombie waste.
