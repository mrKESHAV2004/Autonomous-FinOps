FinOps Glossary

Zombie Waste
Estimated cost of resources that are idle or priced far above similar resources (cost above the peer median, not the whole bill).
BAU Forecast
Business-as-usual projection assuming current patterns continue.
Confidence Level
How much evidence supports a finding: High, Medium or Low, based on which signals fired.
Optimized Forecast
Projected costs after implementing waste removal recommendations.
CostPerUnit
Cost divided by consumption quantity - key efficiency metric.
95% CI
95% confidence interval around forecast values.
